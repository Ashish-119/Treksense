Peak Finder

Raise your phone at the skyline. Named Himalayan peaks — elevation, distance and bearing — get pinned to the real ridge using your camera, compass and GPS.

  • 📷Camera — shows the live skyline so peak labels can be pinned to what you're actually looking at. Never recorded or uploaded.
  • 📍Location — works out which peaks are visible from where you stand, and downloads map data for your area. Stays on your device.
  • 🧭Motion & orientation — knows which direction you're facing, so the right peak lines up with the right part of the sky.

Best held upright, outdoors, away from cars and metal railings.
